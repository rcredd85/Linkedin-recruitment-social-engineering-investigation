# MITRE ATT&CK hypothesis mapping

**Important:** No ATT&CK technique is confirmed in this case. ATT&CK primarily models adversary behavior in computer intrusions; recruitment fraud is not automatically an ATT&CK-detected event.

- **T1598 — Phishing for Information:** Consider only if evidence later establishes an adversarial attempt to elicit information. A request for a resume alone is not sufficient.
- **T1589 — Gather Victim Identity Information:** Potential downstream objective if collection of identity information is established; not observed here.
- **T1566.003 — Spearphishing via Service:** A conceptual comparison for malicious messages delivered through an online service, but no malicious link, payload, or adversary intent is established.

**Current result:** Hypotheses only. Do not count as confirmed detections.
