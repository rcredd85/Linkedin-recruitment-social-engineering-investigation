# Job seeker safety guide

1. Ask for a specific job title, requisition number, employer, pay range, and official listing.
2. Independently verify the posting and recruiter through official corporate channels.
3. Avoid sending identity documents, banking details, one-time codes, or payment before independently verifying the employer and hiring process.
4. Be cautious about pressure to move immediately to encrypted messaging or to pay for equipment or training.
5. Preserve messages and report suspected fraud through the platform's reporting tools; consider reporting to the FTC at https://reportfraud.ftc.gov/.
6. Distinguish warning signs from proof: legitimate outreach can be vague, and suspicious outreach is not necessarily criminal.
